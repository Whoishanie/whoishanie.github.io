01 · About

Background

I'm in my final semester of the MSc Mind, Brain & Behavior program at Justus Liebig University Gießen, finishing my thesis on how lip material appearance and facial dynamics shape trustworthiness judgments, under Prof. Katharina Dobs' Visual Cognition & Computational Neuroscience Lab (VCCN). The work uses Unreal Engine 5 and MetaHuman avatars to render matched matte and glossy lip conditions across static and moving faces.

Before that, I worked in the PACE Lab under Prof. Katja Dörschner-Boyaci, and I currently split my time between the Visual Cognition & Computational Neuroscience Lab (VCCN) and JLU's HRZ as a student assistant. I'm now applying to PhD programs in visual and computational perception.
