01 — About

Background

A short version of where I've worked and what I'm finishing up.

I'm in my final semester of the MSc Mind, Brain & Behavior program at Justus Liebig University Gießen, finishing a thesis on material perception under Prof. Katharina Dobs' Face Perception Lab. The work uses Unreal Engine 5 and MetaHuman avatars to render matched matte and glossy lip conditions, isolating the visual cues people use to judge surface material.

Before that, I worked in the PACE Lab under Prof. Katja Dörschner-Boyaci, and I currently split my time between the Face Perception Lab and JLU's HRZ as a student assistant. I'm now applying to PhD programs in visual and computational perception.
